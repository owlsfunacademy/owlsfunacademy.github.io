Privacy Policy for Owls Promo

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Owls Promo ("the App") is a private content-management app operated by Owls Fun Academy ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App uses when it connects to Pinterest and other platforms.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the Owls Fun Academy team to manage our own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Information we access

  • Basic Pinterest account information for our own account (username, profile, boards).
  • Pins, boards and their analytics that belong to our account.
  • Access tokens issued by Pinterest after we authorize the App.

How we use it

  • To create, schedule and manage Pins and boards on our own account.
  • To research content ideas and view performance of our own Pins.

We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising profiling.

Storage and security

Tokens and data are stored in our private Google account (Google Apps Script and Google Sheets), which only our team can access. Access tokens are never displayed publicly.

Data retention and deletion

We keep data only as long as needed to manage our account. We can disconnect the App and delete stored tokens and data at any time. You can also revoke the App's access from your Pinterest account settings. To request deletion, email us at the address below.

Children's privacy

Although we publish children's books and content, the App is used only by adult team members and does not collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page.
